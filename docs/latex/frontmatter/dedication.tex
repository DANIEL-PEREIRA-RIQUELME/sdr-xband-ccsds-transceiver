I would like to express my gratitude to Prof. Andreas Peter Burg for the opportunity to develop this project within the Telecommunications Circuits Laboratory. My deepest appreciation goes to my co-supervisor, Jonathan Magnin, for his outstanding day-to-day guidance, patience, and invaluable advice throughout our collaboration.

Throughout my studies at EPFL and UGR I had the pleasure to interact with many great lecturers. In particular, Chiurtu Nicolae, José Lizana and Ángel de la Torre left an unmistakable imprint on my work. I have also had the opportunity to belong to many associations with fascinating people, such as the Spacecraft Team within which this project was developed.

These years would not have been the same without my friends — Alex, Guille, Edu (and their amazing girlfriends), Jorge, Marco, Óscar (and also their amazing girlfriends), Marina, Miguel, Migue, Dimitri, Alex, Minas, among others — whose companionship, endless conversations, and all the times we couldn't stop laughing filled this time with joy and made it truly unforgettable. ``Even some of them did the same in 24 hours as I did in a day".

I am especially grateful to my parents and family for their unwavering support, love, and, of course, their money. I don’t think I’d be writing this without them, so all I can say is thank you.
